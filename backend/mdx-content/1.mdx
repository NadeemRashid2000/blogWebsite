# 🏗️ Tower of Hanoi

The **Tower of Hanoi** game consists of **three stacks** (left, middle, and right) and **n round disks** of different sizes.  
Initially, the **left stack** contains all the disks, arranged in **increasing order** from top to bottom.  

The goal is to move all the disks **from the left stack to the right stack**, using the middle stack as **temporary storage**.  

## 🔹 Rules
- You **can only move the top disk** of a stack at a time.
- A **larger disk cannot be placed on a smaller disk**.
- Your task is to **minimize the number of moves** needed to complete the game.
